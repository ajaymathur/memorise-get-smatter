Privacy Policy

Get Smarter: Memory Games · Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version

We don't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers, and the app makes no network connections of its own.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, progress, and settings are stored on your device so the app can show your history and unlock levels.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your scores and progress sync between your devices through your private iCloud database, run by Apple. Only you can access it. We, the developer, can't see it. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you're signed in and choose to share scores, your best scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center nickname, and other players can see them on leaderboards. You can stop sharing at any time in the app's Settings. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the app, or delete the app. To remove synced data, manage the app's data in iCloud settings on your device.
