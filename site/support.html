FAQ

How do I unlock Advanced and Expert?
Reach the target score on the level below. The target is shown on the locked level. If you've played before on another device, you can turn on Settings → Unlock all levels.

Why aren't my scores on the leaderboard?
Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then turn on Settings → Share scores on leaderboards in Get Smarter. Relaxed-timing games and Daily Training are never posted.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything works offline. Scores you choose to share are sent the next time you're online.

My progress didn't appear on my new device.
Progress syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for the app.

Is this a medical or clinical tool?
No. The games are based on research tasks, but the app is for entertainment and education only.
