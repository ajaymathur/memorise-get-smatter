Four quick memory games for iPhone and iPad, based on classic psychology experiments.

Each game has Beginner, Advanced and Expert levels, plus a five-minute Daily Training circuit that adapts to you. You can post scores to Game Center leaderboards if you want to.

Free. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own.

For entertainment and education. Practising these tasks improves performance on them; evidence that this carries over to everyday memory is mixed.
